Before you film

Filming guide

Two short videos for the Falcon Ridge Digital landing page. The VSL plays at the top of the landing page. The confirmation video plays on the page people see right after they book their free strategy call. Both scripts are written for Bryce to film solo.

Non-negotiables

  1. Use the best camera you have. This needs to be the highest quality possible. A recent phone in 4K is fine.
  2. USE A MICROPHONE. A clip-on lav mic on your shirt, or a shotgun mic just out of frame. Never the camera's built-in mic.
  3. Film horizontal, 16:9. Both videos play in a wide frame on the website.
  4. Recreate the setup above. Sit at a desk and talk to the camera, at the same distance as the photo.
  5. Talk like you're talking to a friend. Actually speak to them. Don't go too salesy. Watch the video on site.trainandscale.com before you film.
  6. Take your time. Don't rush or get frustrated. If you think you can do a line better, repeat it until it's your best.
  7. Wear black. Black business-professional for the VSL; dark or black clothes for the confirmation video.
  8. Clean, professional background. Nothing cluttered or distracting behind you.
  9. Use the grid on your camera. Line your eyes up on the line shown below.

Set up the shot

  • Camera on a tripod at eye level, about arm's length to a few feet away, so you're framed from the chest up with a little desk visible.
  • Light your face from the front and slightly to one side: a window or a soft light at about 45°. Keep bright windows out from behind you.
  • Turn off fans and air conditioning, and silence phones. Check the mic level with a test clip before the first take.
  • Look into the lens, not at the screen.

Filming solo

  • Both scripts are written for Bryce to film alone. Every spoken line is yours.
  • If someone else from Falcon Ridge Digital will be on camera with you, ask for together versions before filming day.

How to read the scripts

  • Normal text is spoken. Grey italic lines under each heading are directions. Don't say them.
  • The times next to each section are a guide to pacing, not a hard limit.
  • Film one section at a time. Leave two seconds of silence before and after each take so the edit is clean.

Say the offer exactly as written

  • The call is a free strategy call. Say it the same way every time: “book your free strategy call”.
  • The numbers in the VSL are Falcon Ridge Digital's own published figures. Say them exactly as written.
  • Don't add prices, results, numbers or promises that aren't in the script.
  • If a line doesn't feel natural, ask before changing what it promises. Small wording changes are fine.

When you're done

Send the original video files (through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer, not a text message, which compresses them). Send every take; the best takes are cut together in the edit. Falcon Ridge Digital Confirmation Page Script

About 3½ minutes spoken · one take, six prep points

Bryce on camera — relaxed, professional, direct.

"Hey — it's Bryce from Falcon Ridge Digital.

First off, thank you for booking your free strategy call with us.

Before your call, I want to take just a minute to explain what to expect, so we can make the absolute most of our time together.

This isn't a high-pressure sales call.

It's a conversation about your property — where your bookings or leases come from, what you're paying the middlemen, and where your data is leaking — so we can map out a custom roadmap for you.

And just so it's fresh: here's what we do —

  • We audit your digital footprint to find where you're losing money.
  • We build a lead and conversion engine, so you own your data.
  • We drive direct bookings and leases — through local listings, SEO, paid ads, email and text.
  • And we keep tuning it, using your own data.

All built to raise your occupancy and improve your NOI.

So before the call, here's what I'd like you to prepare.

#1. Please be ready at your scheduled time.

Join at the time, and on the link or number, in your booking confirmation — and make sure you're somewhere quiet where you can give the conversation your full attention.

Treat this like an important business meeting — because it is.

#2. Be on time.

We set aside dedicated time for every owner and operator we talk to.

If something comes up, let us know ahead of time — email help@falconridgedigital.com, or call us at (801) 900-6025 — and we'll find you a new time.

#3. Have a reliable connection.

We want the conversation to be productive, without dropped calls or poor audio.

#4. Be ready to talk about your property.

Specifically, be ready to talk about:

  • What type of property you have — multifamily, RV park, mobile home park, marina, self storage or something else.
  • How big it is — how many units, sites, pads or slips.
  • Your occupancy today, and where you want it to be.
  • Where your new residents or guests come from — your own website, listing sites and OTAs, referrals, or a mix — and whether you track it today.
  • Roughly what you spend on marketing each month.
  • Your website, and the booking or property management software and CRM you use now.

If you have more than one property, think about the one that needs the most help — and how many you have in total.

#5. Think about your goals.

Whether you need to fill a new property fast...

Keep your occupancy consistent...

Get the most out of peak season...

Shift more bookings to your own website...

Or boost your offseason...

We'll tailor the conversation around what matters most to you.

#6. Come with questions.

This call is all about you.

If there's anything you've been wondering about — tracking, local listings, ads, owning your data, or how it all works with the software you use today...

This is your time to ask.

One last thing...

Every month the listing sites keep their cut — and your data — is money you don't get back.

This call helps us find exactly where your property is leaking, and what to fix first.

So come prepared, be open, and let's map out your path to higher occupancy and a stronger NOI.

Thanks again for booking.

I'm looking forward to learning about your property...

Talk to you soon."
